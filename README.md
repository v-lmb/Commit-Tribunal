##  The Sanction: Why Write Commit Messages Correctly?

Here’s why writing clear, structured, and meaningful commit messages actually matters:

- To quickly develop **good writing habits**.  
-  To **know exactly what has been changed** in the code when revisiting it — without confusion.  
-  In **group projects**, so everyone can follow changes and **avoid wasting time** searching.  
-  Because **Hugo reads everything**... and is *very* creative with penalties.  
-  On **personal projects**, after a break, you’ll often forget what you were doing.  
  Well-written commits help you **retrace your thought process**.  
-  **Recruiters** will see your commit history — make it your portfolio, not your diary.  
-  Commit messages are **not** rage rooms, therapy sessions, or tweets.  
-  “**For those who come after**” — a timeless principle.
